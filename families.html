Common questions

A few helpful starting points.

It should be a chance to share what you're looking for, ask questions and learn about the organisation's services and process. You do not need to have every answer ready.

Family collaboration is an important part of person-centred support. Ask the team how it gathers priorities, shares updates and includes the individual in decisions.

Ask about staff qualifications, supervision, consent, individualised goals, how progress is reviewed, and how the provider respects autonomy and communication preferences.

Contact the organisation directly for current fees, funding options, insurance participation and any out-of-pocket costs. These details should be confirmed before starting services.
